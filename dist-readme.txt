CapView - plug-and-play viewer for USB HDMI capture cards
=========================================================

1. Plug in your capture card (and your Switch / console into it).
2. Double-click CapView.exe. That's it - no install needed.

First launch: Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's because
CapView isn't from a big publisher. Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

Controls: move the mouse to show the control bar (hover a button to see
what it does). Double-click = fullscreen, scroll = volume (up to 200%), M = mute,
S = screenshot, right-click = settings.

Nothing showing?
- "Capture card is busy": close OBS / Discord / browser tabs using it.
- "Windows is blocking camera access": Settings > Privacy & security >
  Camera > turn on "Let desktop apps access your camera".
- Still stuck: right-click > Open log file, and send that file to whoever
  gave you CapView.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
